# Magic Item Classification {.magic-item-title}

Valeria classifies magical items by the **power and complexity of their enchantment** and the **mana development required to use them safely**.

There are six classifications:

## **Ordinarius**

The lowest classification.

Its enchantment is completely self-contained and requires **no personal mana**. Anyone can use it if they understand how it operates.

## **Insignis**

More advanced than Ordinarius, but still completely self-contained.

It requires **no Praedari training** and can be used by ordinary people.

## **Eximius**

The first classification that requires mana from its wielder. The user must possess developed mana pathways and sufficient control to connect with the item.

**Minimum Rank: Peritus**

## **Praestans**

Requires greater mana reserves, stronger pathways, and better control than Eximius equipment.

**Minimum Rank: Potens**

## **Singularis**

Extremely advanced magical equipment that depends upon the wielder's **mana density, stability, and resilience**, not merely their ability to supply mana.

**Minimum Rank: Excelsus**

## **Aeternus**

The highest recognized classification. Aeternus items place extraordinary demands upon their wielder and represent the limits of magical craftsmanship.

**Minimum Rank: Supremus**
