Avara is home to **eight major races**: Humans, Elves, Dwarves, Orcs, Halflings, Goliaths, Half-Elves, and Half-Orcs.

Together, they number more than a billion across the continent, though the true population remains uncertain because the number of Orcs living beyond the borders of organized states has never been reliably counted.

They are not divided neatly into eight civilizations. Humans alone possess countless kingdoms and cultures, while Goliaths are scattered across the continent and Orcs live largely among independent tribes. Elves and Dwarves maintain civilizations of their own, Halflings favor smaller communities, and Half-Elves and Half-Orcs have their own histories shaped by others.
