# **Half-Orcs and Half-Elves** {.half-blood-title}

<img class="race-banner" src="../../assets/images/races/half%20orc%20%26%20half%20elf.png" alt="Half-Orcs and Half-Elves of Avara">

## **The Unwanted Future**

Half-orcs and half-elves are treated with prejudice and disdain across much of Avara. In many places, they are seen less as people and more as objects, curiosities, or reminders of something others would rather forget. For them, even receiving basic decency can become a struggle.

There is only one place where half-orcs and half-elves are welcomed openly, without persecution or prejudice, but that is another story. This Codex concerns only how their lineages began. Be warned, these are not tales of forbidden love or innocent infatuation.

For half-elves, the story begins with a darker desire shared between humans and elves.

## **Half-Elves**

Half-elves closely resemble pure-blooded elves, enough that the difference may not be obvious at first glance. The clearest distinctions are their shorter lifespans and lower rate of mana attunement compared to full-blooded elves. Humans sometimes claim they can recognize a half-elf by the way they carry themselves, as they rarely possess the unmistakable pride associated with pure-blooded elves. Elves have another way of knowing. Those sensitive to mana can feel the difference in the weaker quality that surrounds a half-elf.

Human fascination with elven beauty is hardly a secret. Many humans would never openly admit how deeply elves captivate them, but their eyes often reveal enough. Among elves, humans hold a peculiar place as well. Many elves regard humans as lesser versions of themselves, similar enough to be interesting but inferior enough not to be considered equals. Some elves have even kept humans as pets or curiosities simply out of fascination.

From this unhealthy fascination came many half-elven bloodlines.

Almost every half-elf lineage begins with the child of a human and a pure-blooded elf, and the circumstances behind those unions are rarely pleasant. Elven slaves are highly valued among humans, particularly those captured as spoils of war and prized for their appearance. Their beauty turns them into commodities, and many are treated more like possessions than living people.

The elven side is no kinder. Humans have also been kept and used as objects through which elves indulge desires they would never openly acknowledge. Male human slaves are especially sought after in certain Dark Elf societies, where fascination with humans can take forms just as cruel as those found among human slaveholders.

The children born from these relationships are often unwanted by both sides. Humans may look upon half-elves with the same fascination they hold toward elves themselves, treating them as desirable for a time before discarding them when that fascination fades. Elves often see something more shameful: a living reminder of desires they would rather keep hidden. The fact that half-elves possess shorter lives and weaker mana than pure-blooded elves only deepens that contempt.

For many half-elves, the prejudice begins the moment they are born. Some are treated as possessions, while others are ignored entirely. Wherever they go, they may find themselves judged not for anything they have done, but simply for what their existence represents to others.

As unjust as the lives of half-elves can be, their counterparts are often treated even more harshly.

## **Half-Orcs**

If the history of half-elves begins with dark fascination and desire, the history of half-orcs often begins with one word:

**Captives.**

Orc tribes are infamous throughout Avara for warfare, raiding, and their unusually high fertility. Many cities and settlements have fallen to orc raids over the centuries, and when those raids succeed, captives are often carried away unwillingly.

Orc tribes can rise with frightening speed, but they can be destroyed just as quickly. When other armies eventually break those tribes and reclaim what was lost, they sometimes find something few wish to confront among the survivors: women carrying orc children.

What follows is rarely merciful.

Some mothers are killed while the half-orc child is still inside the womb. Others are allowed to give birth before the child is killed afterward. Only a fortunate few are given any chance at life. Some mothers escape and disappear into secluded places where neither their former captors nor their own people can easily find them. Some half-orc children are abandoned. Others survive only to be sold into slavery later.

Those who reach adulthood often carry their orcish blood visibly. Half-orcs tend to develop powerful musculature, and their tusks remain clearly formed even though they are smaller than those of a full-blooded orc. Many also have a reputation for being quick-tempered and easy to anger.

Whether an individual half-orc deserves that reputation often matters very little.

To many civilizations, the first thing they see when they look at a half-orc is the ancestor behind them. The barbarian. The raider. The warmonger. The orc who once burned settlements and carried away captives. A half-orc may have committed none of those crimes, yet they are forced to carry the hatred left behind by those who did.

Because of this, many half-orcs find themselves living as mercenaries or slaves, with very little between the two. Their lives are shaped by persecution for actions they never committed, and many are hated simply for existing.

Even full-blooded orcs offer them little comfort. To many tribes, half-orcs are merely smaller and weaker versions of themselves. In a society where weakness is often despised, there is little reason for the tribe to welcome them.

For much of history, half-orcs moved constantly from one place to another. In that sense, their lives sometimes resembled those of the goliaths, who were likewise scattered across Avara rather than gathered into large settled civilizations.

The difference is that goliaths wandered because it was part of their way of life.

Half-orcs wandered because they had to.

To many people in Avara, half-orcs remain the embodiment of a defiled lineage.
