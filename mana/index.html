    Mana¶

    It's the living force that makes the heart beat, the one that keeps the blood flowing in a newborn child and the old. It is something that cannot be seen nor felt by most people and yet it flows through the air as it sustains the life of every living being.

    Most creatures will never see nor feel mana flow into their body. They can learn about it, know it exists and it will always be an alienated concept in their mind.

    But not everyone is born equal, for some selected few, mana is akin more to an old friend. Each race has a different mana attunement rate, the one with the highest chances are the elves. The people that can feel and see the mana have a tendency to be stronger, faster, smarter and more captivating in presence.

    For those who can use mana, the laws of nature are a challenge in their eyes. Spells that can split the earth with a swift of a hand. Movement of a sword that cannot be seen by the naked eye. A body that is lighter than a feather as it gracefully slides down from the top of a cliff.

    Men and women that were given mana attunement by the world are the foundation of many civilizations. For those who believe a great power comes with greater responsibility, they tend to become the saviour of the world, a pioneer that guides humanity to a better future.

    These people are named Praedari by the empire tongue.

    Praedari¶

    Named bestowed by the common tongue of the empire, a moniker for people who can retain more than the minimum amount of mana needed to live. It is a group of people that are revered and feared by the masses.

    Every praedari always sticks out like a sore thumb among the citizens, there is always an unexplainable shift following them as they enter a room. Disciplines and the way of mana differ from each praedari. Talents and training separate praedari from one another. Some prefer speed faster than sound, some choose the path of invulnerability, and others attune to nature to bend the law of universe itself.

    Individuals like this cannot be left alone. They need to be trained and disciplined to become the best version of themselves and the betterment of society. Praedari that are left alone to their own devices, will never reach their full potential, or worse, they will become a variable that must be removed in the future.

    There is no equality even among praedari, some are just barely stronger than a normal person. Then you avert your gaze to the left, a praedari that can level an entire village with the flick of a finger is laying down relaxed on an open field enjoying the brisk of the sun.

    The praedari looks at you. A thought enters your mind, a simple fact that states your life could be taken away in a blink appears as you see him smiling at you, waving his hand. The smile could be genuine, or not, and that makes things worse. Uncertainty from a person that holds the power to decide whether you and your village would see another sunrise tomorrow will never sit comfortably in your head.

    Being born as a praedari means that you will never have a normal life, either you become someone who is revered, hated or feared.

    The choices are yours, the others will reciprocate in equal measure.

    Mana Reinforcement and Resistance¶

    As a Praedari grows stronger, mana settles deeper into the body, reinforcing flesh, bone, and organs, against attacks with no magical property. Steel can still cut, bullets can still penetrate, and sufficient force can still kill, but the damage will not fully reach a praedari with strong foundation.

    Among the rare and exceptional Praedari, this reinforcement develops into something greater. Their mana becomes dense enough to resist attacks directly, causing ordinary weapons to struggle to reach the flesh beneath.

    Bullets flatten, explosion and fire will only give them warmth, barely touching their clothes. To properly injure an Exceptional Praedari, an attack must carry mana. The stronger the Praedari, the more powerful the opposing mana must be to overcome their protection.

    Against the strongest among them, Mana is not an advantage in a fight, it's mandatory for a chance to wound them.

    Praedari Ranks¶

    The empire divides Praedari into six ranks based on mana capacity, density, control, output, and overall development.

    Tiro¶

    The lowest recognized rank.

    The lowest recognized rank. Most Tiro are first year academy students or newly discovered Praedari. Their mana is still immature, and their control remains incomplete.

    They are considered potential rather than fully trained Praedari.

    Peritus¶

    The first fully developed rank. A Peritus possesses reliable mana control and enough development to use their abilities consistently in ordinary work or combat. Most peristus do not become soldiers. Peristus can be seen scattered around the empire working in civilian professions such as agriculture, industrial production, medicines and many more. They are the foundation that supports the imperial army.

    Many academy graduates or talented students enter the society at this rank.

    Potens¶

    An experienced Praedari whose mana use has become largely instinctive. Potens are seasoned practitioners with greater reserves and control than Peritus. In the military, they form the core of Praedari units.

    Most Potens are veterans that have built mountains of experience in their respective fields.

    Excelsus¶

    An elite Praedari with significantly greater mana and exceptionally refined control. Many Praedari never progress beyond Potens. Those who reach Excelsus are uncommon and often become prominent within their profession. Excelsus also marks the minimum level of mana capable of reliably wounding Eminens and Supremus.

    Within the military, they form the core of Elite Praedari units. Around half of all Excelsus in the empire serve within the military.

    Eminens¶

    An exceptionally rare Praedari whose mana has developed true density. Their mana saturates the body so completely that ordinary weapons struggle to inflict any injuries. At the minimum an Excelsus level is normally required to reliably bypass this protection.

    Most Eminens are cultivated with significant Imperial support and serve as major military assets.

    Supremus¶

    The highest recognized Praedari rank. Their mana capacity, density, control, and output exceed even Eminens by a considerable margin. A Supremus can function as a military force in their own right and may be capable of confronting entire formations alone. They are extraordinarily rare and are usually tied to the Imperial Family, the Custodes Praetoriani, or other major Imperial institutions leaders.

    Supremus has no defined upper limit. Two individuals may hold the same rank while possessing vastly different levels of power.
