    Mana & Praedari¶

    Mana is the living force that makes the heart beat. It keeps blood flowing through the veins of the newborn and the old alike, unseen and unfelt by most people even as it passes through the air and sustains every living being.

    Most creatures will live their entire lives without ever seeing or feeling mana enter their bodies. They may study it, understand that it exists, and witness what others can accomplish with it, yet mana itself will remain an alien concept to them. They know it is there only because the world has given them enough evidence to believe it.

    But not everyone is born equal.

    For a rare few, mana is less like an unseen force and more like an old companion. Every race possesses a different rate of mana attunement, with elves having the greatest natural chance of being born attuned. Those capable of sensing and using mana often grow beyond the ordinary limits of their bodies and minds. They may become stronger, faster, sharper in thought, or possess a presence difficult for others to ignore.

    For those who can truly wield mana, the laws of nature are not absolute rules, but challenges to be overcome. A spell that can split the earth with a sweep of the hand. A sword that move faster than the naked eye can follow. A body becomes light enough to descend from a cliff with the ease of a falling feather.

    People born with this gift have shaped civilizations throughout history. They become warriors, scholars, rulers, healers, explorers, and countless other things according to their own desires. Some believe that greater power brings greater responsibility, and spend their lives protecting others and leading their people towards a better future.

    Others choose very different paths.

    In the tongue of the Empire, those who can sense, retain, and wield mana are known as Praedari.

    Praedari¶

    Praedari is a name bestowed through the common tongue of the Empire, a moniker given to people capable of retaining more than the minimum amount of mana required to sustain life. Across Avara, they are revered and feared by the masses, for even the weakest among them possesses something that most people will never experience for themselves.

    A Praedari rarely blends completely into an ordinary crowd. There is often an unexplainable shift that follows them when they enter a room, something difficult to identify but equally difficult to ignore. Some carry this presence more strongly than others, and those familiar with Praedari eventually learn to recognize that something about the person before them is different.

    The disciplines and ways of mana vary greatly from one Praedari to another. Talent, training, and personal preference shape what each individual eventually becomes. Some pursue speed until their movements surpass what the naked eye can follow, while others strengthen their bodies until ordinary weapons struggle to harm them. Others attune themselves to nature and the forces surrounding it, learning to bend laws of the universe that most people can only obey.

    Individuals with such potential are rarely left entirely to their own devices. They must be trained and disciplined if they are to become the best versions of themselves and use their abilities for the betterment of society. A Praedari who receives no guidance may never reach their full potential. While one who possesses great power without discipline will eventually become a variable that must be removed.

    There is no equality even among Praedari. Some are only slightly stronger than an ordinary person, remarkable enough to stand above the average citizen but hardly powerful enough to reshape the world around them. Then there are those who exist at the opposite extreme, individuals capable of destroying a village while taking a midnight stroll.

    Imagine looking across an open field and seeing one such Praedari lying peacefully beneath the sun. He appears completely relaxed, enjoying the warmth and the breeze like any other person might, yet you know that with little more than a gesture he could level the village behind you. Nothing about the sight is immediately threatening until he notices you watching and turns his attention in your direction.

    He smiles and raises a hand in greeting.

    Perhaps the smile is genuine. Perhaps he means you no harm at all, but the uncertainty is enough to place an uncomfortable thought in your mind. The man waving pleasantly across the field possesses enough power to decide whether you, your family, and everyone in your village will live to see another sunrise. Knowing that someone holds such power will never sit comfortably in the mind of an ordinary person, no matter how kindly that person behaves.

    To be born a Praedari is therefore to lose much of the certainty of an ordinary life. Power attracts attention, and attention brings expectations that cannot easily be escaped. Some will revere a Praedari for what they can accomplish, others will hate them for what they represent, and many will fear them simply because of what they might do.

    What a Praedari becomes is ultimately their choice, but the world will respond to that choice in equal measure.

    Mana Reinforcement and Resistance¶

    As a Praedari grows stronger, mana settles deeper into the body, reinforcing flesh, bone, and organs, against attacks with no magical property. Steel can still cut, bullets can still penetrate, and sufficient force can still kill, but the damage will not fully reach a praedari with strong foundation.

    Among the rare and exceptional Praedari, this reinforcement develops into something greater. Their mana becomes dense enough to resist attacks directly, causing ordinary weapons to struggle to reach the flesh beneath.

    Bullets flatten, explosion and fire will only give them warmth barely touching their clothes. To properly injure an Exceptional Praedari, an attack must carry mana. The stronger the Praedari, the more powerful the opposing mana must be to overcome their protection.

    Against the strongest among them, Mana is not an advantage in a fight, it's mandatory for a chance to wound them.

    Praedari Ranks¶

    The empire divides Praedari into six ranks based on mana capacity, density, control, output, and overall development.

    Tiro¶

    The lowest recognized rank. Most Tiro are first year academy students or newly discovered Praedari. Their mana is still immature, and their control remains incomplete.

    They are considered potential rather than fully trained Praedari.

    Peritus¶

    The first fully developed rank. A Peritus possesses reliable mana control and enough development to use their abilities consistently in ordinary work or combat. Most peristus do not become soldiers. Peristus can be seen scattered around the empire working in civilian professions such as agriculture, industrial production, medicines and many more. They are the foundation that supports the imperial army.

    Many academy graduates or talented students enter the society at this rank.

    Potens¶

    An experienced Praedari whose mana use has become largely instinctive. Potens are seasoned practitioners with greater reserves and control than Peritus. In the military, they form the core of Praedari units.

    Most Potens are veterans that have built mountains of experience in their respective fields.

    Excelsus¶

    An elite Praedari with significantly greater mana and exceptionally refined control. Many Praedari never progress beyond Potens. Those who reach Excelsus are uncommon and often become prominent within their profession. Excelsus also marks the minimum level of mana capable of reliably wounding Eminens and Supremus.

    Within the military, they form the core of Elite Praedari units. Around half of all Excelsus in the empire serve within the military.

    Eminens¶

    An exceptionally rare Praedari whose mana has developed true density. Their mana saturates the body so completely that ordinary weapons struggle to inflict any injuries. Mana of at least Excelsus level is normally required to reliably bypass this protection.

    Most Eminens are cultivated with significant Imperial support and serve as major military assets.

    Supremus¶

    The highest recognized Praedari rank. Their mana capacity, density, control, and output exceed even Eminens by a considerable margin. A Supremus can function as a military force in their own right and may be capable of confronting entire formations alone. They are extraordinarily rare and are usually tied to the Imperial Family, the Custodes Praetoriani, or other major Imperial institutions leaders.

    Supremus has no defined upper limit. Two individuals may hold the same rank while possessing vastly different levels of power.
