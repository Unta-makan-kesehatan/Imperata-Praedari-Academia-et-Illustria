    Wood Elves¶

    The Forest Is Already at Work¶

    Wood Elves are closely attuned to living environments. They learn to read mana moving through forests, rivers, animals, roots, soil, weather, decay, and growth.

    A hunter may sense that something large has entered the forest before finding a track. A druid may notice a grove’s sickness before its leaves change. The signs are there; learning to see them takes time.

    Wood Elven lands are not untouched wilderness. Forests are harvested, hunting grounds are used, waterways are sometimes redirected, and fire may help old woodland renew itself. The limit is how much the land can endure and still recover.

    Wood Elves preserve the forest by knowing how much they can take.

    Restraint Has Its Own Pride¶

    Wood Elves are not ignorant of tools, machinery, or the wealth beneath their lands. They may know how to mine more, clear more forest, or use mana on a greater scale. Their traditions ask what such gains would destroy and whether the land could recover afterward.

    This restraint has protected forests of extraordinary age and mana. It can also become its own kind of arrogance. Wood Elves may mistake another people’s need for carelessness, or judge a difficult compromise as simple greed. Balance is easier to demand when generations before you preserved the land.

    Watch, Mislead, Strike¶

    Wood Elves practice every major discipline of mana, with particular strengths in Divination, Illusion, and Mentamancy. They are known for noticing what others overlook, concealing movement, misdirecting intruders, and influencing attention, emotion, or instinct.

    Their enchanters are especially renowned for ranged weapons. Bows, crossbows, arrows, and throwing weapons may be shaped for accuracy, range, silence, and reliable magical effects.

    A Wood Elf does not always need the strongest spell or the heaviest weapon.

    Seeing first, staying unseen, and striking from a distance may be enough.
