    Half Orcs and Half Elves¶

    The Unwanted Future¶

    Throughout much of Avara, half orcs and half elves are met with prejudice and disdain. In many places, they are regarded less as people and more as objects, curiosities, or living reminders of things others would rather forget. Even the simplest act of decency can be difficult for them to obtain.

    There is only one place in Avara where both races are openly welcomed, free from persecution and prejudice. But that is a story for another time. This Codex concerns only the origins of their lineages. Be warned, these are not tales of forbidden love or innocent infatuation.

    Half Elves¶

    Half elves bear such a close resemblance to pureblooded elves that distinguishing between them can be difficult at first glance. The clearest differences lie in their shorter lifespans and lower rate of mana attunement compared to their pureblooded kin. Humans sometimes claim they can recognize a half elf by their mannerisms, particularly the absence of that unmistakable pride so often associated with elves. Whether there is any truth to this is debatable, but elves have a more reliable means of recognition. Those sufficiently sensitive to mana can perceive the weaker attunement that betrays the human blood within them.

    The fascination humans hold toward elven beauty has never been much of a secret. Even those who would never openly admit such desires often betray themselves through lingering glances and the attention they give to an elf passing through their company. Among elves, however, the fascination takes a different form. Many regard humans as lesser versions of themselves, similar enough to arouse curiosity, yet sufficiently inferior that treating them as equals seems almost absurd. Some elves have even gone so far as to keep humans as pets or private curiosities, simply for the pleasure of possessing them.

    It is from this unhealthy fascination that many half elven bloodlines originated.

    Almost every half elf lineage begins with the child of a human and a pureblooded elf, though the circumstances surrounding their conception are rarely pleasant. Elven slaves have long been highly valued among humans, particularly those captured as spoils of war. Their beauty often determines their worth, with especially desirable captives commanding considerable prices. Many are purchased not for labor, but for the pleasure of their owners, who regard their bodies as possessions to be used as they please.

    The elven side is no kinder. Humans have likewise been enslaved and sexually exploited to satisfy desires their elven masters would never openly acknowledge. Male human slaves are particularly sought after in certain Dark Elf societies, where fascination with humans can become every bit as cruel as the practices found among human slaveholders.

    The children born from these circumstances are frequently unwanted by both races. Among humans, half elves may inherit enough of their elven parent's beauty to become objects of fascination themselves, coveted for a time before being discarded once that fascination fades. Among elves, they are often regarded with shame, living reminders of desires their parents would rather keep hidden. Their shorter lifespans and weaker mana attunement only deepen this contempt, marking them as inferior in the eyes of their pureblooded kin.

    For many half elves, prejudice begins the moment they are born. Some are raised as possessions, while others are neglected or abandoned by parents unwilling to acknowledge their existence. Even those fortunate enough to escape such circumstances may spend their lives being judged not for anything they have done, but simply for what their existence represents to others.

    As cruel and unjust as the lives of half elves can be, their counterparts among the half orcs often suffer an even harsher fate.

    Half Orcs¶

    If the history of half elves begins with dark fascination and desire, the history of half orcs often begins with a single word.

    Captives.

    Orc tribes are infamous throughout Avara for their relentless warfare, frequent raids, and unusually high fertility. Over the centuries, many cities and settlements have fallen to their attacks, with those unfortunate enough to survive often carried away as captives. Among them are women subjected to sexual abuse at the hands of their captors.

    Orc tribes can grow with frightening speed, yet they can be destroyed just as quickly. When opposing armies eventually break these tribes and reclaim what was lost, they sometimes discover something few are willing to confront among the survivors: women carrying the children of orcs.

    What follows is rarely merciful.

    Some mothers are murdered while their children are still growing inside their wombs. Others are permitted to give birth, only for the infants to be killed afterward. Only a fortunate few are given any chance at life. Some mothers manage to escape with their children, disappearing into secluded regions where neither their former captors nor their own people can easily find them. Man half orc children are abandoned, while some survive only to be sold into slavery later.

    Those who reach adulthood often bear the unmistakable features of their orcish ancestry. Half orcs generally develop powerful musculature, while their tusks remain clearly formed, though noticeably smaller than those of pureblooded orcs. They also have a reputation for being quick tempered and easily provoked, though whether an individual half orc deserves that reputation rarely matters.

    To many civilizations across Avara, a half orc is not an individual deserving of judgment, but a creature already condemned by its blood. They see the barbarian, the raider, the warmonger who burned their settlements, slaughtered their families, and carried their women away into captivity. To them, the half orc is no different from the monsters responsible for those atrocities. It matters little whether they have ever committed a crime or even raised a weapon against another. Their orcish blood is considered guilt enough, and they are made to bear the hatred reserved for their ancestors.

    Many half orcs find themselves living as mercenaries or slaves, with few opportunities beyond the two. Persecuted for crimes they never committed, they are often despised for nothing more than the blood they carry.

    Even pureblooded orcs offer them little comfort. To many tribes, half orcs are merely smaller and weaker versions of themselves, possessing enough orcish blood to be recognized, but not enough to earn their respect. Among orcs, where strength is often valued above all else and weakness is met with contempt, half orcs are seldom welcomed as equals.

    For much of Avara's history, half orcs have wandered from one place to another, rarely finding anywhere willing to accept them. In this regard, their lives resemble those of the goliaths, whose communities are likewise scattered across Avara rather than gathered into vast, settled civilizations.

    Yet there is an important difference between them.

    The goliaths wander because it is part of their way of life, a tradition carried through generations. Half orcs wander because they have little choice.

    For in the eyes of many races across Avara, the half orc remains the embodiment of a defiled lineage.
